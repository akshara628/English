That and Those

🔴 Use “that” for one thing that is far from us.

🟠 Use “those” for many things that are far from us.

Look at the pictures. Can you spot the difference between that and those?

That is a hat.
Those are hats.
That is a car.
Those are cars.
That is a balloon.
Those are balloons.

Easy Rule!

🔴 That: One thing that is far from you.

🟠 Those: Many things that are far from you.
