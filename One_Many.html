One and Many

🟠 When we talk about one thing, we just use the word.

🟢 When we talk about many things, we usually add an "s" at the end!

Look at the pictures. Can you see the difference between one and many?

One Book
Two Books
One Ball
Three Balls
One Butterfly
Many Butterflies

Easy Rule!

🟠 One: No "s" at the end.

🟢 Many: Add "s" at the end to show there is more than one.
