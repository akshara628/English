I and You

🟢 I = When you talk about yourself.

🔵 You = When you talk to someone else.

Look at the pictures. Who is talking? Is it I or You?

I am a girl.
You are a girl.
I am a boy.
You are a boy.
I am a student.
You are a student.

Quick Tip!

🗣️ I = Me, the speaker
👂 You = The person I’m talking to
