I, You, He, She, It, We, They

👤 I = talking about myself
🫵 You = talking to someone
👦 He = one boy or man
👧 She = one girl or woman
🐶📦 It = one thing or animal
👨‍👩‍👧 We = me + others
👥 They = other people or things

Let’s look at some pictures and learn how to use each word!

I am happy.
You are my friend.
He is my brother.
She is my sister.
It is a ball.
We are friends.
They are playing.

Summary

🟢 I – One person (me)
🟢 You – The person I talk to
🟢 He / She / It – One person or thing
🟢 We – Me + others
🟢 They – Other people or things
