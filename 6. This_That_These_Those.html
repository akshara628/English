This, That, These, Those

🔵 This – ONE thing that is NEAR you.

🟢 That – ONE thing that is FAR from you.

🟣 These – MANY things that are NEAR you.

🟠 Those – MANY things that are FAR from you.

Look at the pictures. Can you tell if it’s this, that, these or those?

This is a pencil.
These are pencils.
That is a car.
Those are cars.
This is a hat.
That is a hat.
These are balloons.
Those are balloons.

Quick Tip!

👆 This / These = Close to you
👉 That / Those = Far from you

1 thing → This / That
Many things → These / Those
