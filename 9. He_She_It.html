He, She and It

👦 He = for a boy or a man
👧 She = for a girl or a woman
🐶/📦 It = for a thing, animal, or object

Look at the pictures. Who or what is it? Use He, She or It.

He is a boy.
She is a girl.
It is a cat.
He is a teacher.
She is a nurse.
It is a ball.

Quick Tip!

✔️ He → Boy, dad, man, brother
✔️ She → Girl, mom, woman, sister
✔️ It → Animal, toy, place, object
