These and Those

🟢 We use “these” for many things that are close to us.

🟡 We use “those” for many things that are far from us.

Look at the pictures. Can you see the difference between these and those?

These are shoes.
Those are shoes.
These are flowers.
Those are flowers.
These are birds.
Those are birds.

Easy Rule!

🟢 These: Use for many things that are near you.

🟡 Those: Use for many things that are far from you.
